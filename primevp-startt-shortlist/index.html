The take

Prime's 53 cheques are not a sector list. They are one repeated bet: software that sits inside somebody's operating workflow and gets paid by a business. Vertical and function SaaS plus financial-services rails account for 52% of the weighted book; B2B outnumbers B2C 31 to 13; the median company is 2.0 years old at first cheque, and the median founder has 10 years of experience with a 60% IIT/IIM/ISB/NIT/BITS hit rate. Recency sharpens rather than changes it: of the 14 first cheques since Jan 2023, twelve sell to a business (8 B2B, 4 B2B2C). The one visible shift is energy transition and frontier tech: 10.4% of the book overall, but 3 of the last 14 cheques (Metafin, StrainX, AquaAirX).

The hard screen: nothing that has raised more than $500K. Prime writes the first institutional cheque, so a company already carrying a priced round is not the opportunity, whatever else it scores. One healthcare-credit name cleared every other test on this page and came off on that rule alone, at $1.5M across five rounds. Eight of the twelve have raised nothing at all; the other four carry a government grant or an angel cheque under ₹1 Cr.

Top of stack: Reconzo is the closest thing in the database to a second Recko: ecommerce payment reconciliation, built by a Snapdeal senior director with two more IIT-Madras operator co-founders. Flido is the field-operations layer under the lending book Prime already owns through Navadhan and WheelsEye, with Muthoot Finance live and four institutional backers on the cap table.

Methodology. Prime's persona was built from a corrected deal ledger of 53 first cheques (the 52 companies on primevp.in plus AquaAirX, a Jan 2026 deal absent from their page), with entry dates taken as the earliest date attested across Prime's own page payload and press, and company age measured from MCA incorporation where the register resolved an exact name match (30 of 50 attempted), else the researched founding year. Deals since 1 Jan 2023 are weighted 2×. Founder persona is derived from 122 founder LinkedIn profiles, giving years of experience at founding, repeat-founder status, technical role history and institute. All 7,549 founders in the Startt snapshot (691 Live Dealflow, 6,858 Founder Radar, pulled 17 Sep 2026) were scored on nine dimensions (theme, customer type, experience, repeat founder, technical background, institute, company age, geography and traction), normalised by fields actually present, so sparse Radar rows are not penalised for missing data. Every shortlisted company was then checked by hand against its live website for site status, founder attribution and category before it was allowed onto this page.

Caveats. Traction figures marked with a warning are self-reported by the company and were not independently corroborated. A grant or incubation badge is not a priced round and is not presented as one.
