## What these numbers cover

**In scope.** Cell chemistry and materials. Electrode, electrolyte, cathode, anode and separator formulations. Stationary and grid scale storage. Generation of any kind. Transmission, distribution and grid management. Hydrogen production and storage. Fuels. Conversion and power electronics serving generation or grid.

**Out of scope.** Vehicle integration. Battery packs, mounting, enclosures and vehicle thermal management. Charging infrastructure and connectors. Drivetrain, traction motors and regenerative braking. Vehicle side battery management.

## What these numbers cannot tell you
- Applicant origin is the address on the published record, not corporate ownership. A locally registered subsidiary of a foreign group reads as domestic.
- Inventor residence is a residence field, not a nationality field.
- First appearance means first appearance in this financial year, not an applicant's first ever filing.
- Applicant type is read from the applicant name. It is reliable for universities and for names carrying a legal form, and less so for small unincorporated filers.
- Publication is the observable weekly event. The date an application was filed is a property of the record, not something that happened this week.
- A patent is evidence of intent to protect, not of a working product, revenue or a company.
